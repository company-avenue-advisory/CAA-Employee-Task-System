Getting started with the Task & EOD System

Four steps, no installs, no new password — it runs at caa-employee-task-system.vercel.app and signs you in with the CAA Google account you already have.

1Get added to the roster

Send your manager the CAA Google account email you'll sign in with. They add you to the EMPLOYEES sheet with the right role — Employee, Manager, or Owner.

This is the only setup step, and someone else does it for you.

2Open the app and sign in with Google

No separate account, no password — pick your CAA Google account when prompted.

3Land on the dashboard for your role

The app shows a different view depending on what's set in the roster — nothing to configure on your end.

Employee
Today's assigned tasks, plus + Add Work I Took Up for anything you picked up yourself.
Manager
Team dashboard: assign tasks, filter by employee/status/source, EOD roster.
Owner
Everything a Manager sees, plus an Admin tab with company-wide reporting.

4Bookmark it

It's a normal web app — save caa-employee-task-system.vercel.app to your home screen or bookmarks bar. Signing in again later is one click; the session keeps you signed in for 24 hours.

Your daily rhythm as an Employee

✓ Update status and progress on each task as you work through it.
✓ Add a short EOD note on the task — what happened, what's done, what's pending.
✓ Picked up work nobody assigned you? Log it with + Add Work I Took Up so it shows up for your manager too.
✓ Before you sign off, hit Submit Today's EOD. It locks the day's tasks — ask your manager to reopen it if you need to fix something after.
